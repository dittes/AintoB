What is PNM?

PNM is the family of very simple Netpbm image formats: PBM for black and white, PGM for grey and PPM for colour. The files are easy to write by hand or from a script, which makes them popular in research, teaching and Unix tools.

Common uses for PNM

  • Opening images from scientific or command-line tools
  • Creating PPM files for programming exercises
  • Converting plain test images to PNG or JPG
Note: PNM files are uncompressed and therefore large, and support for transparency and metadata is minimal.

Compatibility: Supported by GIMP, ImageMagick, ffmpeg and many programming libraries; not by browsers.

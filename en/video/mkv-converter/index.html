What is MKV?

MKV is the open Matroska container that can hold any number of video, audio and subtitle tracks in one file. It is popular for movies, TV recordings and screen captures from tools such as OBS.

Common uses for MKV

  • Playing OBS recordings on phones, TVs and editors
  • Converting downloaded films to MP4 for wider device support
  • Creating MKV for collections that keep several audio or subtitle tracks
Note: Only the first video and audio track are converted here, and subtitle tracks are not carried over. Many TVs, phones and editors cannot play MKV directly.

Compatibility: VLC, MPV, Plex, Kodi and most desktop players; limited support on iPhone, QuickTime and browsers.

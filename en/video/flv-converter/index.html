What is FLV?

FLV is the Flash Video container that powered online video in the era of Adobe Flash Player. Many old web video downloads, screen recordings and streaming archives still use it.

Common uses for FLV

  • Rescuing old Flash-era videos so they play on modern devices
  • Converting FLV downloads to MP4 for phones and TVs
  • Producing FLV for legacy players that still expect it
Note: Flash was discontinued in 2020 and no browser plays FLV any more. Convert to MP4 or WebM for everyday use.

Compatibility: VLC and other ffmpeg-based players. No browser support since the end of Flash.

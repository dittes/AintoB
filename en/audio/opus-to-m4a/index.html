Why Convert OPUS to M4A?

Converting OPUS to M4A is a common task whenever a file has to work somewhere that expects the other format. Opus is an open, royalty-free audio codec standardised by the IETF that delivers excellent quality at low bitrates. M4A is Apple's container format for AAC-encoded audio. This converter makes the switch locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Common use cases

  • You have a OPUS file and need it in M4A format.
  • The target software or platform requires M4A but your file is in OPUS.
  • Typical use of M4A: Music from Apple Music or iTunes.
  • You want to reduce file size or improve compatibility without using desktop software.
Keep in mind: Some older devices and media players may not support .m4a files and require conversion to MP3.

About the Formats

🎙️

OPUS (Opus Audio)

Opus is an open, royalty-free audio codec standardised by the IETF that delivers excellent quality at low bitrates. It is the codec behind WhatsApp voice messages, Discord, and most modern voice and streaming applications.

Compatibility: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari 17+, Android 5+, VLC and most modern players.

🎵

M4A (MPEG-4 Audio)

M4A is Apple's container format for AAC-encoded audio. It delivers excellent audio quality at low bitrates — significantly better than MP3 at equivalent sizes — and is the default format for Apple Music, iTunes purchases, and voice recordings on Apple devices.

Compatibility: Native on Apple devices, iTunes, QuickTime, and most modern media players including VLC and Windows Media Player.
